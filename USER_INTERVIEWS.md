# User Research Notes

## Target Audience

The product was designed around:
- startups
- developers
- AI-heavy teams
- technical founders

who actively manage multiple AI subscriptions.

---

# Common Problems Identified

Several recurring pain points emerged:

- overlapping AI subscriptions
- unclear monthly AI spend
- duplicated assistant tooling
- unused premium plans
- fragmented workflows

---

# Behavioral Observations

Many users:
- experiment with multiple AI tools
- forget unused subscriptions
- underestimate total spend
- lack centralized visibility

AI tooling adoption often grows faster than operational oversight.

---

# Product Insights

Users responded positively to:

- executive-style reports
- simplified financial summaries
- AI-generated recommendations
- benchmark comparisons

The PDF export feature significantly improved perceived professionalism.

---

# UX Insights

One important realization was that:

structured presentation dramatically affects perceived product quality.

Users trusted:
- clean reports
- hierarchy
- spacing
- executive formatting

more than raw metrics alone.

---

# Future Research Areas

Future interviews would focus on:

- enterprise workflows
- API-heavy organizations
- procurement teams
- finance operations
- AI governance concerns
