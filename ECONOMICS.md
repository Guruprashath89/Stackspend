# Unit Economics

## Current MVP Costs

The current MVP primarily incurs:

- AI inference costs
- hosting costs
- storage costs (future)
- email costs (future)

---

# AI Costs

The MVP uses:
- Groq API

during the prototype stage.

This significantly reduced:
- inference cost
- experimentation friction
- development overhead

compared to traditional paid LLM APIs.

---

# Hosting Costs

Deployment target:
- Vercel

Expected MVP hosting costs:
- minimal during early-stage usage

because:
- serverless architecture
- lightweight backend
- limited persistent storage

---
